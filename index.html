Money Mind Autopilot

Money Mind Autopilot is a private tool that the owner of the YouTube channel The Money Mind uses to publish their own videos.

It reads finished videos from the owner's own Google Drive folder and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel on a schedule, and it reads that channel's YouTube statistics to plan future videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
