Privacy policy

Money Mind Autopilot is used only by the owner of The Money Mind YouTube channel, on their own accounts.

What data it uses

  • Google Drive: it lists, downloads and moves files in the owner's upload-queue folder, and writes status notes on those folders.
  • YouTube: it uploads videos, thumbnails and captions, edits video details and playlists, and reads channel and video statistics (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API).

How the data is handled

  • Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to analyse the owner's own channel.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never published.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • YouTube features are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Statistics stored by the tool are deleted on request through the channel's About page.
